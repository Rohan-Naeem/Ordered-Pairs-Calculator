About this calculator

Ordered Pairs Calculator is a small, focused tool for one job: take an equation or function and turn it into a table of ordered pairs, (x, y), that you can read, copy, or plot.

It was built for students working through algebra homework, parents helping at the kitchen table, and anyone who just needs a quick, accurate table of points without opening a full graphing app.

How it works

You enter an equation using x as the variable, choose a range or list of x-values, and the calculator substitutes each value in, solves for y, and lists the resulting ordered pairs. All of the math runs directly in your browser — nothing is sent to a server.

Why we built it this way

The site is intentionally simple: one clear calculator, a short explanation of what ordered pairs are, and answers to the questions people most often have. No clutter, no sign-up, no unnecessary steps between you and your results.
