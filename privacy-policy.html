Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ordered Pairs Calculator ("we," "us") handles information when you use this website.

Calculator data

The equations and x-values you enter into the calculator are processed entirely in your browser using JavaScript. They are not sent to, or stored on, any server.

Information we may collect

Like most websites, we may automatically collect limited technical information, such as your browser type, device type, approximate location, and pages visited, through standard analytics and hosting logs. This information is used only to understand how the site is used and to keep it running reliably.

Cookies

The site may use cookies for basic functionality and analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; doing so should not affect your ability to use the calculator.

Third-party services

We may use third-party services, such as analytics or advertising providers, that collect information according to their own privacy policies. We do not control how those third parties use the data they collect.

Children's privacy

This site is a general-audience educational tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
